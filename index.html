a letter for Kashvi

My love,

Happy birthday to the person who somehow made ordinary days feel brighter. I wanted this place to begin gently, because that feels the most like you — beautiful without trying too hard, unforgettable without ever needing to ask for attention. So this is a little letter, written for my love, to hold a few things I never want to forget: the easy laughter, the quiet moments, the ridiculous ones, the comfort of your presence, and the way even simple memories feel softer when they belong to you. I hope today reminds you of how loved you are, how deeply appreciated you are, and how easy it is to celebrate you. And if words ever fall short, I hope the rest of this little world says some of it for me.

Always yours.
