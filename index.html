a letter for Kashvi

My love,

I still remember how shy I was, that I couldn’t even talk to you. It was our annual function practice, you were all sitting in a round sabha, and I was standing near you. Then you looked up, pulled a chair, and told me to sit. My heart was racing. It was just a few weeks after we met, and in that moment I knew you were my life.

Some of my happiest hours were in the field, just sitting with you and talking while our friends played football. I was in a bad place then, and nothing was making me happy. Being near you gave me so much joy. It was the first time in months that I smiled without having to fake it.

I love your lips. I love your face when you’re sad, and when your lips come out because you want something. How cute can someone be? I can’t describe your eyes. Looking into them feels like that nostalgic feeling of the person you were two or three years, of being nothing and everything at once.

This past year has been the most magical of my life, and you’re the reason. I have never felt this alive. You’re the only one I need for the rest of my life. I love you only, and will always no matter what. And no one can take this from us.

Always yours.
